HealthPlatform: a health summary from a medical wearable

An intelligent system that supports health monitoring: it joins biometric data from a medical IoT wristband with the user's own medical documents, and uses AI to generate personal health recommendations.

Goal

Turn data from a medical wristband into readable, personal health insights.

What I built

A React/TypeScript platform with a RAG assistant over the user's own medical documents.

Highlight

Voice in and out: speech recognition for symptoms, spoken answers back.

React 18TypeScriptSupabaseLangflowAstraDBOpenAIGroqElevenLabs
SiDLY bandbiometric data from the medical IoT wristband
SupabasePostgreSQL, auth and storage for medical documents
RAG in Langflowdocuments embedded in AstraDB, retrieved per question
LLMsGPT-4 and Groq for answers and recommendations
VoiceWhisper to listen, ElevenLabs to speak back

Objectives

  • IoT device integration: connecting the SiDLY medical wristband.
  • AI assistant with RAG: a chatbot that draws on the user's medical documents.
  • Recommendation system: machine-learning algorithms that generate personal health advice.
  • Voice analysis: speech recognition and symptom analysis from audio recordings.
  • Medical documentation: storing and analysing the user's medical documents.
